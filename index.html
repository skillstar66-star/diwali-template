Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of sarees and jewellery does ROKEA by RK offer?

We curate handpicked premium Soft Silk sarees, Dubion silk, Glow Viscose, and heritage collections. In jewellery, we offer handcrafted antique gold finish, temple jewellery, and bridal necklace sets crafted for weddings, festivals, and celebrations.

How does the Custom Blouse Stitching service work?

You can click "Design Your Blouse" on our website to submit your design ideas, neckline/sleeve preferences, and measurements through our quick Google Form. Our master tailors will reach out on WhatsApp/Phone to confirm your requirements and tailor a bespoke blouse that fits you like a second skin.

How do I provide my measurements for blouse stitching?

You can fill in your chest, waist, shoulder, and sleeve measurements in inches in our custom order form. If you're unsure about taking measurements, message us on WhatsApp at +91 70103 94051, and our stylists will guide you with a measurement tutorial.

Do you offer worldwide delivery and what is the timeline?

Yes! We ship across India and worldwide with trusted courier partners. Standard saree and jewellery orders are typically dispatched within 24–48 hours and delivered within 4–7 business days across India. Custom stitched blouses require 7–10 days for precision tailoring before dispatch.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept 100% secure payments via Google Pay, PhonePe, UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking (via Razorpay), and Direct Bank Transfers.

How can I reach customer support or book a boutique appointment?

You can call or WhatsApp us anytime at +91 70103 94051 or visit our boutique at 11/4, Bharathi Park 4th Cross Rd, Saibaba Colony, Coimbatore. You can also message us directly on Instagram @rokeabyrk.
